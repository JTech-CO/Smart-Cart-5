# Smart-Cart-5 1.0.0

Prototype integration revision; measurement and electrical release remain on hold.

- Replaced the D4 full-extrusion concept with a 900×600 commercial platform, local spreader plates, raised wheel adapters and two suspended battery trays.
- Preserved the Motor-Bracket Rev A BREP construction and asymmetric interface; used a rigid 180-degree rotation for the opposite side rather than a reflected hole pattern.
- Switched the main processor to Raspberry Pi 4B 8GB and reserved the V8 RBP-002 enclosure space.
- Added handle-mounted E-stop and separate manual reset, with visible external handle routes and hinge service loops.
- Rebuilt all 50 cable/harness paths against the new structure; included real grommet holes, cable saddles and removable-cover openings.
- Added same-source 3D/2D physical routing, electrical relationship diagrams and connection length CSV.
- Enabled negative-elevation orbit, automatic ground hiding/restoration and responsive field-of-view compensation.
- Added real solid STEP/FCStd/BREP exports, a FreeCAD import macro, generation code and machine-readable QA reports.
- Corrected coplanar motor-can/end-cap faces, label orientation and shadow self-acne in the final render pass.
- Kept uncertain wheel, motor-body conflict, cart-frame and additional electrical-component dimensions explicit. No firmware, physical load test, energization or manufacturing approval is claimed.
